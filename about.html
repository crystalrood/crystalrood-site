A little about me

Hi, I’m Crystal.

I make things, follow ideas, and keep notes on what I learn along the way.

This site is a place to gather recipes and notes that might otherwise live in scattered folders, open tabs, and half-filled notebooks. It’s a small recipe box I can return to.

I care about thoughtful details, useful things, and leaving room for a project to evolve. This collection is new, so consider it a living draft.

What guides this space

01

Make it useful.

Keep enough context that future me, or someone curious, can actually cook from what’s here.

02

Keep the notes.

Record the tweaks, timing, and small details I’ll want to remember the next time.

03

Leave room to grow.

Start small, publish the work, and let the shape of this site emerge over time.
